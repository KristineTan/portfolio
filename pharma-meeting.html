E-Learning · Pharmaceutical Leadership

Meeting-Design Competencies for New Managers

An e-learning program for a Greater Boston pharmaceutical leader, equipping newly promoted managers with frameworks for high-impact meetings and cross-departmental effectiveness.

Project Overview

I designed and developed this e-learning program for a Greater Boston pharmaceutical leader to equip newly promoted managers with essential meeting-design competencies. The course enables managers to strategically determine when a meeting is required and provides a framework for conducting high-impact interactions.

By filling a critical gap in universal managerial training, this solution directly enhances cross-departmental efficiency and leadership effectiveness.

Learner Persona

Promoted new managers in a pharmaceutical company, mainly with backgrounds in medical or science fields and moderate social skills. Technology-fluent and fast learners.
