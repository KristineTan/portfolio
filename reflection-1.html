T127 / Reflection 01

Personal Learning Goals & Initial Project Considerations 🌱

My hopes, learning goals, and early thinking about a project of interest as I begin this transformative course in instructional design.

✨ Course Vision

I hope and believe that T127 will be a transformative course that allows me to translate my knowledge into tangible practice while learning through the process of creation. As an emerging instructional designer, I am actively building my aesthetic and technical skills. In addition, I have several personal learning goals for this course:

  • 📚 Develop a strong theoretical foundation in instructional design to better understand and evaluate what constitutes high-quality design.
  • 🛠️ Gain hands-on experience across the design process, including conducting learner analysis, defining learning objectives, designing interactive activities, prototyping modules, and aligning content with learners' needs.
  • 🤝 Engage in collaborative conversations with instructional designers (IDs), subject matter experts (SMEs), and other stakeholders to better understand real-world project workflows.
  • 🤖 Experiment with AI in instructional design, both by using AI to support development and by exploring how designers can effectively augment their work with emerging tools.

🔍 Project Selection

My experience building student communities through my nonprofit mental health organization and my background in student engagement have shaped my commitment to student-centered design and learning communities. At the same time, I have developed a strong interest in educational technology and AI. As an educator, one of my long-term goals is to help learners understand how to apply frameworks and customize digital tools to meet their own needs.

For this course, I would like to step outside my comfort zone by either developing an online course for the LEAP initiative or exploring how AI can be integrated into learning design. Through these projects, I hope to gain experience implementing instructional design in real-life institutional contexts while actively examining the role of AI in the design process.

💡 Developing a pilot course particularly excites me because I want to create a tangible product that real learners can interact with and respond to. Receiving feedback from different stakeholders will help me evaluate the effectiveness of my design decisions, identify areas for improvement, and deepen my understanding of how design choices impact learner experience and engagement.

Ultimately, this project will allow me to move beyond theoretical learning and toward iterative, evidence-informed practice as an emerging instructional designer. 🚀
